Tools

Web tools for subtitles, on-screen text and translation.

  • Subtitle Studio

    Turns an English video into a dubbing CSV. It transcribes the video, sets up a shared Google Doc for translation and proofreading, then exports the finished text as timed sentences.

    srt-csv.yury-kuzmin.workers.dev

  • Supers OCR

    Reads titles, captions and lower thirds from a video and exports them to Excel with timecodes, with an optional translation column. The video stays on your computer.

    supers-ocr.yury-kuzmin.workers.dev

  • Streamlit Translator

    Translates text with your choice of AI models and prompts.

    app-translation-cabm95pqfunorsr4wsqp8q.streamlit.app

  • Translation Feedback

    For proofreaders. Compares a volunteer's draft translation with the final version, explains the likely reason for each change, and produces a report to send back to the volunteer.

    translation-feedback-7t9kpjlb827s82qzerwzu9.streamlit.app
